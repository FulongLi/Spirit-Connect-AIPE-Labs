We respect your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and why.

## What we collect
- Contact details you submit (e.g., name, email) via forms or email.
- Basic analytics (aggregate usage, pages visited). No personally identifying analytics are published.

## How we use data
- To respond to enquiries and provide services.
- To improve the website and our offerings.
- To comply with legal obligations.

## Newsletter (AIPE Updates)
- If you subscribe at /subscribe/, we process your email address, the language you chose, the time of your consent and, for spam prevention, your IP address.
- Our legal basis is your consent. We use double opt-in: nothing is sent until you confirm from the email we send you.
- Subscriber records are held by our newsletter provider, Buttondown (Buttondown, LLC, United States), which processes them on our behalf and records opens and clicks to report delivery. Transfers outside the UK rely on the provider's standard contractual safeguards.
- Every email contains an unsubscribe link, and you can withdraw consent at any time by writing to us. After you unsubscribe we keep only a suppression record so you are not re-added by mistake.
- Subscribing is independent of any AIPE Community account.

## AIPE Community accounts
- **Account data**: your email address, a password hash or GitHub sign-in identifier (handled by our authentication provider), and sign-in records. Your email address is never shown publicly.
- **Public profile**: your username, display name, profile image, biography, interests, affiliation, links and published contributions are public. Fill in only what you are happy to publish.
- **Content you submit**: drafts are visible only to you and to moderators until published; published contributions and comments are public. Moderation decisions, notes and reports are kept in an audit record visible to moderators.
- **Legal basis**: performance of our agreement with you to provide the Community (Terms of Use), and our legitimate interest in keeping it safe (moderation, rate limiting and abuse prevention).
- **Retention and deletion**: you can download your data and delete your account at any time from My account. Deletion removes your account, profile, profile image, contributions and comments; moderation records keep the decision but no longer identify you. Backups held by our provider expire on its standard schedule.
- **Separate from marketing**: creating an account never subscribes you to email updates.

## Service providers
- Supabase (Supabase, Inc.) hosts the Community database, authentication, file storage and server functions.
- Buttondown (Buttondown, LLC) sends AIPE Updates to subscribers.
- GitHub (GitHub, Inc.) authenticates you if you choose to sign in with GitHub, and hosts this website.
- Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.) may provide spam protection (Turnstile) on forms.

Some providers process data outside the UK; we rely on their standard contractual safeguards for such transfers.

## Sharing
We do not sell your data. We only share with service providers who process data on our behalf (e.g., hosting, email) under appropriate agreements, or when required by law.

## Retention
We keep data only as long as necessary for the purposes above, or as required by law.

## Your rights
You may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your personal data.  
Contact: **info@spiritconnect.co.uk**

## Security
We use HTTPS, access controls enforced in the database, and industry-standard safeguards. No method is 100% secure, but we continuously improve protections.

## Updates
We may update this policy and will post the new version here with a revision date.
