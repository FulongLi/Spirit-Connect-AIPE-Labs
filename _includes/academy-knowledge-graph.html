{%- comment -%} Data-driven engineering knowledge graph (assets/knowledge-map.js), drawn as an explorable network in the style of the Blog knowledge map: the card, canvas, controls and tooltip reuse the blog-graph styles. Params: lang; scope (optional subject key). Without a scope the whole graph is shown; with one, that subject's topics plus their direct prerequisites and dependents in other subjects. Topics come from _data/knowledge_graph.yml and subjects from _data/academy.yml. Lessons are generated AIPE Academy pages filtered to the page language, so a topic without a lesson in this language is shown as planned and links nowhere. The topic list below the graph is the no-script and screen-reader fallback, rendered from the same data. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign kg_lang = include.lang | default: page.lang | default: 'en' -%} {%- if kg_lang == 'zh' -%}{%- assign kg_lp = '/zh' -%}{%- else -%}{%- assign kg_lp = '' -%}{%- endif -%} {%- assign kg_t = site.data.i18n[kg_lang] -%} {%- assign kg_lessons = "" | split: "" -%} {%- for candidate in site.pages -%} {%- if candidate.academy_page and candidate.lesson_status and candidate.lang == kg_lang -%}{%- assign kg_lessons = kg_lessons | push: candidate -%}{%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- assign kg_nodes = site.data.knowledge_graph.nodes -%} {%- if include.scope -%} {%- assign kg_stage = site.data.academy.stages | where: "key", include.scope | first -%} {%- assign kg_title = kg_t.academy_subject_map_title | replace: '%s', kg_stage.title[kg_lang] -%} {%- assign kg_intro = kg_t.academy_subject_map_intro -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign kg_title = kg_t.academy_map_title -%} {%- assign kg_intro = kg_t.academy_map_intro -%} {%- endif -%}
