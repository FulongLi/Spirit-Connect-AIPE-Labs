The AIPE Community is a place to share engineering tools, research ideas and technical writing about AI for power and
energy engineering. These guidelines apply to everything you publish here, together with the
[Terms of Use](/legal/terms/) and [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy/).

## What belongs here
- **Engineering tools**: AI engineering agents, AIGC and simulation tools, open-source utilities, datasets and design
  resources, with honest statements of status, licence and limitations.
- **Frontier ideas**: research ideas, open problems and hypotheses. Say what is proposed and what is known, and how it
  could be tested. An idea is not a result.
- **Technical articles**: tutorials, notes, experimental findings and design experience. Cite your sources.

## What does not
Advertising and spam; plagiarism or content you have no right to share; confidential or export-controlled
information; personal data about others; unsafe instructions presented without the necessary safety context;
harassment, discrimination or personal attacks; malware or links to it; claims of validation you cannot support.

## Review
Every contribution is reviewed by a moderator before it is published. Review checks scope, clarity, links,
licensing and conduct. It is **not** an engineering, safety or scientific validation, and publication does not mean
AIPE endorses the work. Moderators may approve, ask for changes or decline, and may unpublish published work if
problems are reported. You will see the reason with your contribution. To ask for a decision to be reconsidered,
email info@spiritconnect.co.uk.

## Community and official content
Community contributions are always labelled as such. They are not added to the AIPE Hub, Frontiers, Academy,
Registry or Blog automatically. The AIPE team may invite you to have a contribution curated into one of those
sections; that is a separate editorial decision made with you.

## Ownership and licensing
You keep ownership of what you contribute. By submitting it, you confirm you have the right to share it and grant
Spirit Connect a licence to publish its text on aipel.co.uk under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), so others can reuse it with attribution. Projects you link
to keep their own licences.

## AI assistance
You may use AI tools, but you are the author and are responsible for accuracy. Disclose AI assistance in the
contribution form. Automated, unsupervised posting by agents is not allowed.

## Profiles and badges
Profiles are public apart from your email address. "Official AIPE contributor" and "Community moderator" badges show a
role on this site; neither is a professional qualification or verification.

## Reporting
Use the report link on a contribution or comment, or email info@spiritconnect.co.uk. Reports are seen only by
moderators.
